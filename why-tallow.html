What is tallow?

Tallow is fat rendered from animals such as cattle and sheep. It can be used for frying, roasting and sautéing. Its flavour and texture depend on the animal source and rendering process.

How can I use it?

Start with a small amount and adjust to the dish you are preparing. Try familiar Kenyan favourites like chapati, potatoes and stews. As with other cooking fats, portions matter.

How do I store it?

Keep sealed in a cool, dry place and use a clean, dry spoon. Refrigeration can be helpful in warm conditions. Follow packaging instructions for product-specific storage guidance.
