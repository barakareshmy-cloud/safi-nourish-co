Open `index.html` in Chrome/Edge. Use the navigation to browse separate pages: Home, Our Story, Shop Tallow, Bulk Orders, Why Tallow?, Gallery and Contact.

This site includes the original Safi product photographs from the supplied banner and gallery thumbnail crops from the Facebook screenshots you provided.
